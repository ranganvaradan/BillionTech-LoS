SCF Working Capital Programme — Sample Credit Policy

1. Underwriting criteria
Bureau score must be at least 650.
Live unsecured loans must not exceed 6.
Bureau enquiries in the preceding 3 months must not exceed 21.
Annual GST turnover must be at least ₹5 crore.
Business should have operated for at least 3 years.
Banking turnover should be at least 75% of GST turnover.
No cheque bounce during preceding three months.
Cheque bounces in the preceding twelve months must not exceed 6.
CC utilisation must be less than 95%.
DSCR should be minimum 1.25.
Interest coverage should be minimum 1.5.
Debt to equity must not exceed 2.
TOL/TNW must not exceed 7.
ITR income must be at least ₹3,00,000.
PAT must be positive.

2. Limit and product
Standard ticket is ₹50 lakh; maximum exposure shall be ₹1 crore with deviation.
Facilities are sized at 25% of turnover.
Program limit and rate cap apply as per product schedule.
Eligible SCF structures and tenor are defined in the product note.

3. Documents required
Copy of PAN, GST returns, bank statements and audited financials must be submitted.
Buyer NOC and authority documents where applicable.

4. Portfolio controls
Per-anchor concentration and per-borrower concentration caps apply.

5. Servicing
SMA classification, NPA norms, grace period and restructuring follow the servicing policy.

6. Narrative
This policy document shall be read with the authority statements and definitions for the purpose of illustration.

7. Manual judgement
Management quality should be satisfactory.
Promoter experience must be at least 10 years.
