DEMO POLICY / VALIDATION SAMPLE
KYC & Eligibility

This is a demonstration / validation sample for Decision Policy authoring.
It is not a real lender KYC policy and is not production authority.

KYC & Eligibility

• PAN must be verified for all applicants.
• GSTIN must be available for company borrowers.
• Company borrowers must have valid CIN and GSTIN.
• Video KYC is mandatory for loans above ₹5 lakh.
• If PAN name differs from the application name, the case must be referred to KYC Review.
• If KYC information is incomplete, credit underwriting must not proceed.
• Physical KYC may be used where Video KYC cannot be completed and the policy permits it.
• Bank account ownership must be verified before sanction.
• CKYC download should be completed where available.
• Aadhaar OTP verification is required for individual borrowers when configured.
• Udyam verification is required for MSME applicants when Udyam is provided.
• PEP screening must be completed for all applicants.
• Company authorised signatory must be verified.

Credit Underwriting

• Bureau score must be equal or above 650 for scored applicants.
