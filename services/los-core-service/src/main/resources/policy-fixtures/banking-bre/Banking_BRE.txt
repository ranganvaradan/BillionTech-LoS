 
Fields Required in Excel Report 
- Average Daily Balance of last 3 months. 
- Average monthly transaction for last 3 months. 
- Average Daily QR Settlement for lats 3 months. 
- Average Monthly Settlement count for last 3 months. 
- Inward Cheque / ECS / ENACH Returns both in percentage and number of returns 
separately. 
- Intercompany / Within Merchant group transactions. 
- Loans disbursed by Financial Institutions in last 3 months with the name of the Bank 
/ Financial institution. 
- Party wise Large credits with name and amount of transaction. 
- EMI bounces in last 3 months. 
- Any Debits / Credits with respect to online gaming to be shown separately with 
number & value of transactions in last 3 months. 
 
Banking BRE Rules 
- Last 3 months Average Daily Balance should be equal or above the proposed EDI 
amount for Starter Loans Product. 
- One fifth of last 3 months Average Daily Balance should be equal or above the 
proposed EDI amount for DigiLeap Loans Product. 
- Last 3 months monthly average transactions must be minimum of 20 transactions for 
DigiLeap Loans Product. 
- One tenth of last 3 months Average Daily Settlements should be equal or above the 
proposed EDI amount for Smart Switch Product. 
- Last 3 months monthly average number of settlements must be minimum of 20 
Settlements for Smart Switch Loans Product. 
- One fifth of last 3 months Average Daily Balance should be equal or above the 
proposed EDI amount for Reboost Loans Product specific to loan amount above 
Rs.60,000. 
- Last 3 months monthly average transactions must be minimum of 30 transactions for 
ReBoost Loans Product specific to loan amount above Rs.60,000. 
- Inward Cheque Return of 5% if transactions are more than 100 in last 3 months or 5 if 
the transactions are less than 100 for all the bank statement based products. 
- Any Loans disbursed in last 3 months to be removed from Average Daily Balance 
calculation. 
- Any credits from online gaming to be removed from Average Daily Balance calculation. 
- Any bulk deposition by merchant which is more than 10 times of average depositions in 
last 3 months to be removed from Average Daily Balance calculation.
